About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Southern California, advised by Prof. Weihang Wang. My research spans software engineering and software security. I develop techniques for identifying and mitigating security and reliability issues in software systems. I am also interested in evaluating and improving the reliability of language models for code understanding and software engineering tasks.
