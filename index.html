About

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Southern California, advised by Prof. Weihang Wang. Prior to this, I obtained my M.S. in Computer Science from USC and my B.Eng. in Computer Science and Technology from Tsinghua University.

My research interests lie in Software Engineering and Software Security, with a focus on applying program analysis and testing techniques to identify and mitigate security and reliability issues in software systems. I am also working on evaluating and enhancing the reliability of language models for code understanding and software engineering tasks.
